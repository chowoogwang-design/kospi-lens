DATA & CALCULATION GUIDE

Methodology.

01 · Universe definition

All issuers on the KOSPI main board that can be matched to a DART corporation code and have latest reported total assets strictly greater than KRW 200bn. One row per issuer's common share class. ETPs, preferred shares and duplicate issuer rows are excluded. Issuers without verifiable financial statements are not assumed eligible.

02 · Daily market fields

Price: latest available KRX daily close. Trading volume: shares traded on that exact session. Market cap: KRX's published market capitalization for the listed common share class, shown in KRW billions. 1D change: KRX close-to-close percentage move. Price/volume/market cap are date-aligned.

03 · P/E and earnings basis

P/E = listed common share-class market cap ÷ LTM net income attributable to owners

LTM net income = last completed annual FY net income + current fiscal YTD net income − prior-year matching fiscal YTD net income. For example, FY2025 + 2026 H1 − 2025 H1. For an issuer without a newer interim filing, FY net income is displayed and marked FY, not incorrectly described as LTM. Loss-making companies have P/E shown as N/M.

04 · P/B and total assets basis

P/B = listed common share-class market cap ÷ latest equity attributable to owners

Assets and equity come from the latest filed balance sheet, generally consolidated (CFS). If a company has only separate statements (OFS), separate figures are used and labelled. Total assets are a point-in-time balance sheet measure, not LTM. Negative or unavailable book equity produces N/M / a dash.

05 · Important interpretation caveats

For groups with separately listed preferred shares, the common share-class market cap does not represent total company equity market value. Thus P/E and P/B are indicative and may differ from Naver Finance / Bloomberg. Different fiscal year-ends, accounting restatements, banks / insurance accounting, and exceptional earnings can also distort comparability. No undisclosed NCI or net income proxy is substituted when parent-attributable figures are missing.

06 · Industry segmentation

Broad sectors are approximated from the company's OpenDART Korean Standard Industrial Classification (KSIC) code, not official GICS sectors. Source-of-truth 5-digit KSIC is available on each row's tooltip where reported. Some conglomerates may classify differently from your preferred economic exposure.

07 · Data freshness and setup

The dashboard reads a saved daily snapshot prepared by the Python refresh script. Real data requires both KRX and OpenDART API keys and a successful python -m app.refresh --live refresh. Set up a daily scheduler on your deployment for automatic updates. The demonstration mode never represents its synthetic values as real market quotes.
